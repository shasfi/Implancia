Orthopedic Implants

Implancia specializes in manufacturing reliable orthopedic implants, ensuring optimal support and stability for patients undergoing joint replacement and reconstruction surgeries.

Trauma Solutions

Our trauma solutions focus on durable and effective devices for fracture fixation and stabilization, providing surgeons with confidence in critical surgical situations.

Custom Manufacturing

Implancia offers tailored manufacturing solutions, working closely with healthcare providers to create specialized implants that meet unique patient and procedural requirements.

Manufacturing & Quality

Every Implancia implant is produced under strict quality management systems and precision manufacturing controls trusted by surgeons, hospitals, and distributors worldwide.

Precision CNC Manufacturing

Implants are machined on high-precision CNC equipment with tight dimensional tolerances, ensuring consistent quality across every production batch.

Quality Management System

Our manufacturing processes follow a documented Quality Management System aligned with ISO 13485 requirements for medical device production.

Material Traceability

Every implant batch is traceable from raw material (medical-grade titanium and 316L stainless steel) through to final sterile packaging.

Inspection & Testing

In-process and final inspection procedures verify dimensional accuracy, surface finish, and mechanical properties before release.

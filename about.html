About Implancia

Your trusted source for advanced orthopedic and medical device solutions.

The Story Behind Implancia’s Success

Implancia is a locally owned business based in Gujranwala, Pakistan, specializing in the manufacturing of orthopedic and medical device implants.

We offer a wide range of high-quality products designed for various surgical applications, ensuring that our customers receive only the best.

Founded with a vision to improve medical care, Implancia started its journey in medical device manufacturing with a small but passionate team dedicated to quality and reliability.

We proudly serve a diverse clientele, including hospitals, clinics, and surgical centers, delivering custom solutions that cater to their specific medical needs.
